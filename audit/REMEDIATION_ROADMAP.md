# Remediation roadmap

Effort is a rough engineering estimate including regression tests. IDs link to detailed repros and compatibility notes in the [master findings register](AUSGEGEBEN_MASTER_AUDIT.md#verification-matrix-and-findings-register).

## Essential work

| Order | Change | Effort | Dependencies and risk | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AUS-201. Keep the original text for strict validation in web expense and recurring inputs. Use an explicit locale grammar for supported grouping and decimals. | 0.5–1 day | Shared parsing contract and DE/EN fixtures. Low risk to stored data. Previously saved amounts need user review before any correction. | A paste of `1.234,56` is either accepted as €1,234.56 under the DE grammar or rejected visibly. Minus, overflow and excess precision never become a different accepted amount. |
| 2 | AUS-102. Give offline edits/deletes a durable outcome across Android sign-out. Surface server acknowledgment, pending state and explicit discard separately. | 2–4 days | Existing pending-create journal and auth/cache lifecycle. High coordination risk. Bind any replay to the original UID and preserve idempotency and historical revisions. | Edit/delete offline, restart, attempt sign-out and switch accounts, then reconnect. Acknowledged operations survive, retained operations replay for their owner, and explicit discards are visible. |
| 3 | AUS-103. Roll back preferences against a transactionally read live revision, model absent snapshots explicitly, and finish the operation only after the preference state is restored. | 2–3 days | Both clients, strict rules and journal compatibility. Medium risk from old active journals and concurrent writes. Keep existing snapshots readable. | Test future-dated live revision, absent snapshot prefs, competing writer, and transport failure at the final batch on both clients. The journal must accurately reflect incomplete work. |
| 4 | AUS-101. Make category-type migration completion prove all references are consistent, with a resume path for legacy unfinished migrations. | 2–4 days | Existing staged migration and Firestore access-call limits. Medium-to-high migration risk. Detect current mismatches before tightening rules. | Direct clients cannot finish a migration with an old-type linked expense. Existing valid states remain editable, and an interrupted migration resumes safely. |
| 5 | AUS-202/203. Continue recurrence backlog on a bounded and cancellable schedule. Evaluate atomic authority for newly created receipts while retaining legitimate receipts when their expenses are edited or deleted. | 3–5 days | Shared receipt identity and restore support for historical receipts. Medium compatibility risk. Preserve legacy consumption evidence and canonical IDs. | Test 21+ due occurrences, date rollover, restart, restore, receipt-only write, and two clients. Existing legitimately consumed occurrences remain consumed. |
| 6 | AUS-204. Use matching elapsed-day cutoffs for comparisons and derive pace from elapsed spend. Keep the full-month headline on its existing period definition. | 1 day | Shared parity fixture and local-date/time policy. Low data risk. Historic chart interpretation changes only where future rows distorted elapsed metrics. | At Nov 12 with Oct 10 = 100, Nov 10 = 100 and Nov 28 = 400, the same-days delta is zero, the full-month total is 500, and the elapsed spending projection is 249.90 under the existing cent-rounding policy. |
| 7 | AUS-401/402. Resolve and validate one release commit, make release listing fail closed, and verify the tag at publish. | 1–2 days | GitHub tag/ruleset settings and CLI access. Medium operational risk from rejecting moved tags or unavailable APIs. | Isolated workflow tests cover moved and annotated tags, name collisions, empty successful release lists and partial or failed API responses. |
